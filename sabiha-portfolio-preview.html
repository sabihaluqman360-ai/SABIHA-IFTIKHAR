About me

Who is Sabiha?

A fashion and beauty copywriter. Gold medalist in English. I write ads, emails and landing pages that get read to the last line.

My Story

I studied English Literature at Government College University, Faisalabad, and finished as a gold medalist. After that I trained in direct response copywriting in the Copywriting Dominance Program and started writing for clients in January 2026.

How I Work

I start with your customer, not your product. Who is she, what does she scroll past, what makes her stop? Then I write. My clients find me through referrals, so every project has to be good enough to pass on.

Why Fashion & Beauty

Most fashion and beauty ads sound the same. Glowing, flawless, must-have. I write the kind a shopper actually reads, with a real hook and a clear reason to buy.
